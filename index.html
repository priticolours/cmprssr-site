The problem

Raw is wonderful right up until you own three of them.

A CinemaDNG frame runs 50–80 MB. A working shoot is hundreds of gigabytes, the card fills halfway through the day, and the backup you skipped is the one you need. The usual fix — handing footage to a generic compressor — throws away the raw latitude you actually paid for.

Frame 50–80 MB

Cards fill mid-shoot

At 24 fps a single reel crosses 300 GB before lunch. You end up deleting footage to keep shooting, which is the one move that costs you real money.

Reel 300–600 GB

Backups don't happen

Nobody backs up 600 GB twice. The reel exists on one card in one bag, and the drive you meant to copy it to is still formatted.

Then you compress it

The grade goes flat

Generic video compression throws away the highlight and shadow latitude that makes raw worth shooting. The picture survives. The look doesn't.

How it works

Point at a folder. Pick a level. Get gradeable DNGs.

cmprssr drives Adobe DNG Converter in parallel batches, reports the ratio from real tile payload bytes, and verifies every output before it calls the job done.

01

Load a folder

Recursive or not. cmprssr scans for CinemaDNG and R3D, reads each file's compression tag, and estimates the output honestly before you commit a single run.

02

Pick a level

Three tiers, no marketing language between them. Already-lossless folders are passed through untouched so the output reel stays complete.

03

Run and verify

Batches of 16 across max(2, cores / 2) workers. Every output DNG is parsed back — tiling, crop size, required tags — and a failure falls back to the original.

Quality levels

Three tiers, and exactly what each one does.

Every tier writes real DNG. All three keep your raw grading controls in Resolve — the difference is how hard the payload is squeezed and what gets left in the file.

Default Recommended

Lossless JPEG with a medium embedded preview and fast-load data. No quality loss, widest support across editing apps, and smooth scrubbing in the NLE. The honest ratio lands around 1.65–2.6× depending on the footage.

-c -p1 -fl

Lossless

Lossless JPEG with no embedded preview at all. Zero quality loss and the smallest lossless result cmprssr can produce — the one to reach for when you're archiving masters rather than editing.

-c -p0

High Compression

Adobe's lossy DNG, with preview and fast-load retained. The smallest files by a wide margin and the only tier with a visible quality trade-off. Still fully raw in Resolve — best for proxies and oversampled footage.

-lossy -p1 -fl
Requirements

What you need before it runs.

OS

macOS 13 Ventura or newer. Native app — no browser, no runtime, no command line.

ENG

Adobe DNG Converter. cmprssr detects it and offers to install it for you if it's missing.

DISK

Roughly 2× your source size free at the destination, so the run never stalls mid-batch.

RAW

CinemaDNG and R3D folders. Other raw formats pass through the converter unchanged.
